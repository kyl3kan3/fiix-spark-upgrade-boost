# Free preventive maintenance checklist

> Turn recurring maintenance into a schedule your team can actually follow. This preventive maintenance checklist defines the asset, task, frequency, acceptance criteria, responsible role, and next due date in one portable sheet.

Canonical URL: https://maintenease.com/templates/preventive-maintenance-checklist

## Included fields

- **PM task** — One observable action per row.
- **Frequency** — Daily, weekly, monthly, quarterly, annual, or meter-based interval.
- **Acceptance criteria** — What a pass looks like and when to create corrective work.
- **Assigned role** — The job role accountable for completion.
- **Last completed** — The evidence date for the previous cycle.
- **Next due** — The next scheduled date used to manage compliance.

## How to use it

1. **Start with critical assets** — Build the checklist first for equipment whose failure affects safety, production, customers, or a large repair bill.
2. **Write pass-or-fail criteria** — Replace vague instructions like 'check belt' with a measurable inspection and a clear corrective-work trigger.
3. **Schedule from the last completion** — Keep last-completed and next-due dates together. Review overdue work weekly and adjust intervals when evidence supports it.

## FAQ

### What should a preventive maintenance checklist include?

Include the asset, location, task, frequency, procedure or acceptance criteria, assigned role, last completion, next due date, status, and notes.

### How do I choose a preventive maintenance frequency?

Start with the manufacturer recommendation, regulatory requirement, asset criticality, operating hours, and failure history. Adjust only when your records show the interval is too short or too long.
