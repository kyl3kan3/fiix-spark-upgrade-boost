# MaintenEase vs MaintainX

> Flat-fee pricing that doesn't scale with headcount.

Canonical URL: https://maintenease.com/compare/maintenease-vs-maintainx

MaintainX is a popular work-order and procedure app billed per user per month, with a free tier for very small teams and paid tiers that unlock reporting, PMs, and analytics. MaintenEase covers the same everyday workflow — work orders, assets and history, preventive maintenance, inspections, mobile access, and reporting — but charges one flat monthly fee for the whole company. This page lays out where the two products genuinely overlap, where MaintainX is the better fit, and exactly where the flat-fee model starts to win on cost.

## The short answer

Both products are cloud CMMS tools built around mobile work orders: a request comes in, it becomes a work order, a technician completes it on their phone, and the asset keeps a service history. If you are comparing them, you are almost certainly not choosing between "can it do work orders" — both can.

The real decision is pricing model and breadth. MaintainX bills per user per month, with capability gated by tier, and is strongest when your work is procedure-driven — repeatable checklists, SOPs, and safety forms that many hands run identically. MaintenEase bills one flat monthly fee for unlimited work orders and assets, and includes AI predictive maintenance, energy/power usage tracking, a no-login public request portal, and free onboarding on the plan you buy.

MaintenEase Pro is $129/mo flat. Against a publicly listed ~$21/user/mo tier, the two are roughly even at about six paid seats, and MaintenEase is cheaper above that. Below six seats, per-seat can be cheaper — and MaintainX's free tier cheaper still.

## Cost as your crew grows

At a publicly listed ~$21/user/mo: 5 users ≈ $105/mo, 8 users ≈ $168/mo, 15 users ≈ $315/mo, 25 users ≈ $525/mo. MaintenEase Pro stays at $129/mo across all of those; Business is $299/mo.

Two costs people forget to model: seasonal or part-time technicians (a line item per seat, which pushes teams to share logins), and view-only users — supervisors, office staff, and contractors who check status weekly but still consume a seat on most per-seat plans.

Run your own headcount in the [CMMS cost calculator](https://maintenease.com/cmms-cost-calculator), which also shows the range where per-seat pricing wins.

## Where MaintainX is genuinely the better choice

Pick MaintainX if your team is one to four paid users — the per-seat math favours you, and their free tier covers a very small operation. Pick MaintainX if procedure and SOP libraries are the centre of your work; it is built around standardised digital procedures and that is a real strength. It is also the safer pick if you need a large prebuilt integration marketplace, multi-language field crews, or enterprise procurement and security-review depth from a larger vendor.

## Where MaintenEase pulls ahead

- **Cost predictability** — one flat fee, month-to-month, so every technician, supervisor, and office user can be on the system.
- **Included, not gated** — AI predictive maintenance scoring, energy/power usage tracking, digital inspections and checklists, a public request portal with no login, an AI assistant over your own data, and an agent/MCP API.
- **Free, hands-on onboarding** — we import your assets, open work orders, and PM schedules and build your first reports with you.

## What comparison pages usually leave out

- **Requester and view-only seats.** Ask exactly who counts as billable. MaintainX advertises unlimited free requesters; MaintenEase has no seat count at all.
- **Annual vs monthly.** Advertised per-user rates usually assume an annual commitment. MaintenEase's listed prices are month-to-month.
- **Tier gating.** With tiered per-seat products, the price you compare often isn't the tier you end up on. List the three features you need, then find the tier containing all three.
- **Exit cost.** Ask both vendors how you export assets, work order history with attachments, and PM definitions. MaintenEase exports to CSV/Excel on demand.

## FAQ

### How much does MaintenEase save vs MaintainX?

MaintainX's listed plan is around $21 per user per month, so a team of 8 runs about $168/mo. MaintenEase Pro is a flat $129/mo for the whole team — the gap widens with every technician you add. Figures are based on publicly listed pricing as of 2026; check each vendor for current rates.

### At what team size does MaintenEase become cheaper than MaintainX?

Against a publicly listed ~$21/user/mo tier, six paid users is about $126/mo versus MaintenEase Pro at a flat $129/mo — effectively break-even. Below that, per-seat is cheaper; above it, the flat fee wins and the gap grows with every hire.

### Is MaintenEase a good MaintainX alternative for a small team?

Under about five paid users, MaintainX's per-seat pricing (or its free tier) may cost less. MaintenEase Starter is $49/mo flat, so it's competitive for small teams that want unlimited users, no seat management, predictive maintenance, and a public request portal included.

### Do requesters or view-only users cost extra on MaintenEase?

No. There is no seat count, so supervisors, office staff, and part-time technicians cost nothing extra, and requests can arrive through a public portal link with no account.

### Does MaintenEase have a mobile app for technicians?

Yes. Technicians view assigned work, add notes and photos, complete checklists, and close work orders from their phone, and the asset keeps the full history automatically.
