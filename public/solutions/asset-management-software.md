# Asset Management Software that turns your asset register into a working system

> Register, schedule, maintain, and report on every asset — in one place.

Canonical URL: https://maintenease.com/solutions/asset-management-software

MaintenEase is asset management software for the teams who maintain physical equipment. Register each asset with core identifiers, location, status, purchase date, hierarchy, photos, and attachments. Connect recurring calendar work, corrective work orders, parts, labor, cost, and predictive-risk records so the asset register supports day-to-day maintenance decisions.

## Benefits

- **One source of truth** — Every asset, every work order, every PM, every part — in one place. No more reconciling spreadsheets at month-end.
- **Better-supported capital decisions** — Use recorded work, cost, downtime, repeat failures, and risk factors as inputs to repair-or-replace review.
- **Reviewable history** — Keep asset details, attachments, and maintenance activity together so an authorized reviewer can trace the available record.

## Features

- **Core asset register** — Capture name, model, serial number, purchase date, location, status, hierarchy, description, image, and attachments.
- **Recurring work per asset** — Create calendar-based recurring work and checklists against the equipment record; do not assume native meter-trigger scheduling.
- **Work orders and parts** — Every repair, inspection, and part swap is recorded against the asset — building a complete service history.
- **Documents and history** — Attach warranty or contract documents and review related work without claiming an automatic renewal-alert feature.
- **Cost and reliability inputs** — Record labor, parts, downtime, repair frequency, and risk factors for internal reporting; finance owns depreciation policy.
- **Company and role permissions** — Use the application's tenant and role controls, and validate any site-specific access requirement before rollout.

## FAQ

### How is this different from a CMMS?

Asset management is one of the core capabilities of a CMMS. MaintenEase is a full CMMS — asset management, work orders, PMs, parts, and reporting — sold under whichever name fits how you think about the problem.

### Can I track depreciation?

The current core asset record does not publish a built-in straight-line depreciation report. Use exported maintenance and asset data with the approved values and useful-life rules in your finance system.

### Does it work for non-physical assets?

MaintenEase is designed around maintainable physical equipment. Do not rely on this page as a claim for software-license, certification, contract-renewal, or non-physical-asset workflows without validating them first.
