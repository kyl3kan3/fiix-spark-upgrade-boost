# Preventive Maintenance Software that stops failures before they cost you

> Schedule maintenance by date, runtime, or mileage.

Canonical URL: https://maintenease.com/solutions/preventive-maintenance-software

Use a calendar schedule for work due on a date. On Pro and Business, link a PM checklist to an asset meter, set the service interval, and enable automatic work orders. When a technician records a reading at or beyond the interval, MaintenEase automatically creates a work order for that service cycle. Completing the checklist starts the next interval.


## Benefits

- **Fewer avoidable breakdowns** — Put recurring inspections and service on a visible schedule, then compare failures before and after each PM interval so the team can adjust the work based on its own history.
- **More visible workload** — Upcoming and overdue work gives supervisors a clearer view of the schedule before the day begins.
- **Reviewable records** — Completion dates, notes, attachments, checklists, labor, and parts can be retained with the work and asset history.

## Features

- **Calendar schedules** — Create recurring work by day, week, month, or year and associate it with the relevant asset and owner.
- **Meter-based schedules** — For a pump serviced every 500 operating hours, link the pump's hour meter to its checklist and set the interval to 500. Record the current meter value on the asset to check whether service is due.
- **Procedures and checklists** — Use work-order instructions and recurring checklists to capture steps, pass/fail items, notes, and attachments.
- **Documented completion** — Keep the due date, status, assignee, completion record, and supporting evidence together for later review.
- **Schedule health insights** — See which PMs are overdue, which assets keep failing between PMs, and where intervals should change.

## FAQ

### Can we import our existing maintenance data from a spreadsheet?

Free onboarding and data-import help are included. Provide a representative file first so the team can confirm which asset, work-order, and schedule fields can be mapped without implying that every legacy format imports automatically.

### What happens if a PM is missed?

The due work remains visible with its date and status so supervisors can review and reassign it. Teams should define their own overdue and compliance rules rather than assume the software supplies a regulatory conclusion.

### What is preventive maintenance software?

Preventive maintenance software schedules recurring inspections and service against each asset, generates the work orders automatically when they come due, and records completion so PM compliance can be measured and audited.

### How is preventive maintenance software different from a general CMMS?

PM scheduling is one module inside a CMMS. MaintenEase ships both: the preventive schedules described here plus the asset register, work order queue, inspections, and reporting they depend on.
