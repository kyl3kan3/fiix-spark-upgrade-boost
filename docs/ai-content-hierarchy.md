# MaintenEase AI content hierarchy

Updated: August 28, 2026

## Canonical roles

| Route | Role | Primary intent |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `/mcp` | Product and integration page | Verify the live MaintenEase MCP endpoint, OAuth flow, tools, and setup surfaces. |
| `/learn/agentic-cmms` | Educational pillar | Understand agentic CMMS architecture, governed actions, evaluation, and rollout. |
| `/learn/maintenance-mcp-server` | Technical explainer | Understand the role and controls of an MCP server in maintenance software. |
| `/learn/cmms-for-chatgpt` | Client-specific explainer | Evaluate how an authorized AI client can use CMMS tools. |
| `/learn/ai-maintenance-assistant` | Assistant use-case explainer | Evaluate retrieval, drafting, summaries, and human oversight. |
| `/learn/ai-work-order-automation` | Workflow explainer | Evaluate request-to-work-order preparation and confirmation boundaries. |
| `/learn/agentic-maintenance-workflows` | Examples page | Compare concrete read, draft, and confirmed-action workflows. |
| `/learn/ai-native-cmms` | Category explainer | Evaluate the emerging product category without treating the label as a standard. |
| `/learn/equipment-risk-scoring` | Decision-model explainer | Rank attention using explainable maintenance evidence. |
| `/learn/predictive-maintenance-without-sensors` | Data-readiness explainer | Start risk and trend work with history and manual readings. |

The product page and educational pillar are the two hubs. Supporting pages link back through related-term navigation; the pillar also links directly to the product page. New articles must serve a distinct intent and link to the appropriate hub.

## Consolidation guardrail

Do not merge or redirect these routes based on title similarity alone. Review at least four weeks of Google Search Console query-to-page data after deployment. Consolidate only when two pages repeatedly compete for the same material query set and one page has a clearly stronger fit. Preserve a permanent redirect and update internal links when consolidation is supported by that evidence.

## Claim and citation guardrail

Product claims must match the current product catalog and MCP documentation. Emerging-category claims must be presented as definitions or evaluation guidance, not as proof of MaintenEase capability. Prefer primary standards, product documentation, government guidance, and direct vendor sources; label announcements as announcements. Do not create doorway pages, copied roundups, synthetic testimonials, citation swaps, or pages whose only purpose is to repeat a keyword variation.
